How to read this
The default rates are SDG&E bundled summer total electric rates effective April 1, 2026: TOU-DR1 69.572¢ / 47.505¢ / 38.773¢ and EV-TOU-5 80.292¢ / 50.584¢ / 12.852¢ for peak / off-peak / super off-peak. This is a sensitivity model, not a bill-grade tariff engine. It intentionally ignores charges that are usually similar between plans, including fixed monthly/base service charges, taxes, climate credits, TOU-DR1 baseline adjustment credits, SDCP-vs-bundled generation differences, and exact monthly true-up mechanics.
